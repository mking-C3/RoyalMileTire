Where We Run in the Ottawa Area

Common Breakdowns We See on I-35

The I-35 stretch between Olathe and Ottawa carries heavy freight in and out of the KC distribution footprint. The mix of older pavement transitions, summer heat, winter freeze cycles, and crosswinds south of town produces a familiar list of failures: tread punctures from debris near merge ramps, blown trailer markers and tail lights after rough miles, glad hand seal failures on cold mornings, and intermittent electrical faults in tractor harnesses worn by vibration.

Knowing the corridor means we show up with the parts that actually fix the problem the first time.
